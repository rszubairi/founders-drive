Why we do it this way

We don’t roast founders to bring them down. We challenge them to move faster.

The signature of Founders Drive isn’t that a startup got roasted. It’s that a founder opened the business to scrutiny, took honest feedback, acted on it, and came back to show what changed.

Roasters move past generic criticism to name why a company is slow, where the approach is weak, and what the founder must change or accelerate.

The Reality Check framework

Six angles. Every one uncomfortable.

Business

Is the problem real? Will customers pay? Is pricing viable? Is the model sustainable?

Product

Right problem? Too complex? Are customers actually using it?

Market

Is the target market realistic? Is the ICP too broad? Is the market ready?

Go-to-market

Is acquisition repeatable? Sales cycles too long? Is CAC sustainable? Who’s the economic buyer?

Fundraising

Genuinely fundable at this stage? Is the ask supported by traction?

Execution

What’s slowing the company down? What to stop, start or accelerate?

From criticism to action

Every point resolves to a next step.

No finding leaves the room as an opinion. Each is pushed through the same structure until it names a person and a date.

WHY
Target market is too broad and the sales cycle runs 9–12 months.
WHAT
Narrow the ICP.
HOW
Run a 90-day pilot with 20 target customers.
WHO
Two operators, one potential customer, one relevant mentor — introduced by Founders Drive.
The 30 / 60 / 90-day founder journey

The roast is day one. The point is day ninety.

EVENT DAY
Pitch, Reality Check and verdict — live, in front of the room.
DAY 7
Final Action Plan — measurable, time-bound, agreed with the founder.
DAY 30
Progress update against every issue raised.
DAY 60
Results and experiments update — what worked, what didn’t.
DAY 90
Reality Check 2, a progress score, and a published case study.
The signature format

You Said / We Did

What the panel challenged. What the founder changed. What happened. Every founder tracks each issue as Implemented, Partially, Testing or Rejected — with evidence.

You said

“Your CAC only works if the 9-month sales cycle comes down.”

We did

Cut the ICP to mid-market fintech, ran a 20-account pilot, closed 6 in 47 days.

Status · Day 90

IMPLEMENTED  sales cycle now 41 days average.
